The Problem

Blood shortages cost lives every day

India faces a 3 million unit blood deficit annually. Without a centralized system, patients and families are left scrambling during emergencies.

🚨
No Centralized System
Blood banks operate in silos. Patients can't easily find the right blood type in their city.
⏱️
Critical Time Delays
Every minute counts. Searching for donors manually in emergencies wastes precious time.
📍
Geographic Barriers
Hospitals and donors are unconnected. Location-based matching simply doesn't exist.

Our Solution

BloodBond bridges the gap

We built a smart, fast, and compassionate platform that connects every donor to every patient who needs them — in real time.

Instant donor registration with blood type profile
One-click blood request system for emergencies
Smart donor matching using location + blood type
Live map showing nearby donors & blood banks
Notification alerts for urgent blood needs
Verified donor profiles with donation history

Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Who can donate blood?
+
Anyone aged 18–65, weighing at least 50kg, and in good health can donate blood. You should not have donated blood in the last 3 months. Our registration form checks your eligibility automatically.
How safe is the blood donation process?
+
Blood donation is entirely safe. We connect you with certified, WHO-approved blood banks and hospitals only. All equipment used is sterile and single-use. The process takes about 30–45 minutes.
How quickly will I find a donor after submitting a request?
+
Our system sends instant notifications to matching donors in your area. For critical requests, you can expect responses within 15–30 minutes in metro cities. Response times vary by location and blood type availability.
Is my personal data safe on BloodBond?
+
Absolutely. We use Firebase Authentication and Firestore with strict security rules. Your personal contact information is only shared with verified requesters and never sold to third parties.
Which cities is BloodBond currently available in?
+
We currently operate in Delhi, Mumbai, Bangalore, Chennai, Hyderabad, Kolkata, Pune, Ahmedabad, and 100+ other cities. We're expanding rapidly. If your city isn't listed, register anyway — we'll notify you when we launch there.
Is BloodBond free to use?
+
Yes, BloodBond is completely free for donors and patients. Our mission is to save lives, not make a profit from emergencies. We are funded by NGOs, CSR grants, and government health programs.
